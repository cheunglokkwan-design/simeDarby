# Competitor table (for the deck)

Answers "why not just use Google?" before a judge asks. Check each competitor
claim against its source page before it goes on a slide.

| Existing tool | What it does | What we do that it doesn't |
|---|---|---|
| **Google Solar API** | Covers Malaysia since March 2026, but residential areas only, at BASE (lowest) quality. Paid, one building at a time | Malls, factories and car parks; a whole township at once; CREAM matching; SDP 2030 framing; shows how far each number can be trusted |
| **Trexon.my** | Commercial service. Claims 4,176 industrial roofs mapped across Malaysia's six biggest industrial parks (1.385 GWp). Paid reports | Whole townships (homes, shops, malls, schools), not only industrial parks; open data; community and SDP angle |
| **EarthPV** (Open Energy Transition) | Open project that detects *existing* rooftop solar from free satellite images | Finds where solar *should* go, how much it saves and how to fund it |
| **SOLACE, RoofWatt, Rooftop-Solar-Potential, Roofpedia** | Research pipelines: pvlib plus irradiance data; some add neighbourhood maps or an equity angle | Interactive 3D, tied to Malaysian policy (ATAP, CREAM) and to SDP's targets |

The research pipelines also show that pvlib with PVGIS or NASA irradiance is
the accepted method, which is what our yield model uses.

## Our differentiators, one line each

- **Whole township:** every roof type plus car parks.
- **Honest data:** every building records where its outline, type and height came from.
- **Policy-aware:** ATAP export rules and caps, and CREAM leasing with the 9 sen access charge, are built into the numbers.
- **Grid-aware:** distance to the nearest mapped substation for every site.
- **Storage siting alongside solar:** none of the tools above offer it.
- **Open and free:** built entirely from open data, so SDP can rerun it on any new township.

## What we must not claim

- Integration with SolarGIS or HelioScope (both paid). Say "exports to" only once export formats exist.
- "Bankable" numbers. Ours are pre-feasibility; bankable needs a site survey and an independent engineer.
